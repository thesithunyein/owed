# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Everyone participating in the Owed project — issues, pull requests, discussions,
code review, or any other project space — is expected to keep the interaction
professional and good-faith.

The benchmark, in one line: **disagree with the work, not the person.**

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a good environment:

- Critique the code, the data, or the claim — with specifics, not adjectives.
- Assume competence and good faith in others, including when they are wrong.
- Accept that being corrected in public is a normal cost of working in public.
- Respect that this project makes falsifiable claims about live chains and backs
  them with reproducible evidence; demand the same standard of every claim,
  including your own.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Insults, personal attacks, or harassment of any participant, in any channel.
- Trolling, bad-faith argument, or publishing others' private information.
- Sustained disruption of discussions after being asked to stop.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional workplace.

This list is not exhaustive; the standard above is the rule, the lists are
illustrations.

## Scope

This standard applies inside all project spaces (repository, issues, pull
requests, linked chats) and whenever an individual represents the project in
public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report a violation to **<sithunyein@users.noreply.github.com>** or by opening a
private GitHub security/contact report on
[thesithunyein/owed](https://github.com/thesithunyein/owed). Reports are handled
by the maintainer, kept confidential, and reviewed with the reporter's identity
protected where they ask for it.

The maintainer may edit, delete, or refuse content; warn, temporarily mute, or
permanently ban a participant; and publish the reason when the violation was
public. Bad-faith reports are themselves a violation. Anyone asked to stop a
behaviour is expected to comply immediately, pending review.

This project adopts the intent of the [Contributor Covenant][covenant], version
2.1; where this document and the Covenant differ, this document governs.

[covenant]: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
