Evidence, method and reproduction

Verification

The registry is also deployed to Solana devnet: 42WwVtPQ…FCLV, in this transaction. The settlement suite behind the numbers below - a 4:1 split minting every holder's delta, a replayed claim refused, the vault swept to zero - runs against a throwaway validator on every push, and has settled on that devnet deployment too - a green end-to-end CI run: 13 signed transactions covering one split and one cash distribution, from initialize_asset through settle_action(dividend), with a short register and a replayed claim refused on the way.

Method

Effective multipliers are recomputed in your browser from the raw scaledUiAmountConfig state published in the feed, using the same rule the program uses - now >= newMultiplierEffectiveTimestamp ? newMultiplier : multiplier. Sizes and prices cancel out of the error factor, so the multiplier gap is the whole story at any position size.
